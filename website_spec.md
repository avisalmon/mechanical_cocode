# Website Specification: Text-Based Mechanical Description Languages and Co-Coding for Mechanical Design

## Purpose
This website will serve as a comprehensive, professional, and accessible resource supporting the research on text-based mechanical description languages and AI-assisted co-coding for mechanical design. It will present the research findings, provide tutorials, link to relevant tools and resources, and foster community engagement.

## Structure & Navigation
The website will feature a clean, modern design with intuitive navigation. The main page will present a summary of the research, and users can explore deeper content via tabs (or navigation menu) dedicated to specific aspects of the topic.

### Main Sections (Tabs)
1. **Home / Executive Summary**
   - Overview of the research topic
   - Key findings and motivation
   - Visual summary (e.g., infographic or hero image)

2. **Historical & Conceptual Background**
   - Timeline of text-based mechanical design languages
   - Key milestones and influential projects
   - Comparison to traditional CAD and HDLs
   - References and further reading

3. **Survey of Current Solutions**
   - Overview of major tools and languages (OpenSCAD, CadQuery, Modelica, etc.)
   - Feature comparison table
   - Links to official sites, documentation, and open-source repositories
   - Case studies from industry and hobbyist communities

4. **AI-Assisted Co-Coding**
   - Explanation of co-coding and LLMs in mechanical design
   - Examples of AI-generated CAD scripts
   - Links to relevant AI tools (GitHub Copilot, etc.)
   - Research papers and experiments

5. **Tutorials & Guides**
   - Step-by-step guides for getting started with OpenSCAD, Modelica, CadQuery, etc.
   - Sample projects and code snippets
   - Downloadable resources
   - Video walkthroughs (optional)

6. **Challenges & Future Directions**
   - Discussion of current limitations and open research questions
   - Integration with traditional workflows
   - Opportunities for further research and development

7. **Community & Resources**
   - Links to forums, discussion groups, and maker communities
   - List of recommended books, articles, and videos
   - Contact form or feedback channel

## Features
- Responsive design for desktop and mobile
- Professional, readable styling (separate CSS file)
- Tabbed navigation or sidebar menu
- External links open in new tabs
- Search functionality (optional, for larger content)
- Downloadable research report (PDF)
- Embedded code samples and diagrams
- Accessibility considerations

## Technical Stack
- HTML5, CSS3 (separate stylesheet)
- JavaScript (separate file, for interactivity)
- No backend required for initial version (static site)
